# Sharing a website with a client

Client access lets someone review one website’s publication status and open its connected domain. It does not let them edit the website, download files, read application logs, manage billing, or browse other projects.

## For the website owner

1. Open the website from your portfolio and choose **Clients**.
2. For a new account during the private launch, ask the operator to approve the client’s email for registration first. Approval does not give them access to your workspace.
3. Enter the email under **Invite client by email** and choose **Send invitation**. This queues an email containing a link that expires after seven days. Pending does not prove email delivery.
4. The client registers if needed, verifies their email, signs in, then accepts the invitation. Their website appears under **Shared with me**.
5. Use **Revoke invitation** to cancel a pending link. Use **Remove access** to end accepted client access. Access is checked again on each request; already displayed information cannot be recalled.

For an existing verified account, **Existing verified account (no email)** grants access immediately without sending an invitation. Team roles are different: workspace members have access to every website in that workspace according to their role. Client access does not restrict someone’s existing Team permissions.

Each website supports up to 20 clients and 20 pending invitations. Invitation creation and acceptance both check the client limit. Wait at least one minute before sending another invitation to the same person; a website can queue up to 50 invitations per day. A replacement invalidates the previous link.

## For the invited client

Use the same email address that received the invitation. If you do not have an account, create one after operator approval and follow the verification email. Then sign in and reopen the original invitation email to accept website access. You can use **Shared with me** whenever you return.

If the link has expired, been revoked, or the sender no longer owns the workspace, ask the owner for a new invitation. Forwarding the link to another account does not grant that account access.

If you want a website update, contact your developer. Read-only client access does not include publishing or editing controls.

## Organizing your portfolio

Open a website, choose **Settings**, and enter a **Client label**, such as a
company name. Choose **Save client label**. The label appears on the website
card and can be found using the portfolio search. Leave it blank and save to
remove it. Website owners and developers can edit labels; workspace viewers
can read them.

Labels are internal to your workspace. They do not invite someone, grant access,
transfer ownership, change billing or rename the website or its domain. Invited
clients cannot see these labels in **Shared with me**. Use **Clients** when you
want to grant or remove review access.
